About Love In Action

Founded by Kay Krause in July 2025, Love In Action supports detainees at the Chase County Detention Center in Kansas, including 120 ICE detainees and 28 other detainees.

Through compassion, encouragement, and practical support, Love In Action helps detainees stay connected while providing resources that bring comfort and hope.

Availability Notice: Go Bags will NOT be available until December 1, 2026.

Love In Action will provide a Go bag for the Detainees that are being deported. If you or a loved one needs one, please click on the link below to fill out the application. If you are eligible, a Go Bag will be delivered to the Chase County Detention Center and will travel with you through your deportation process. You will receive a bag upon reaching your final deportation destination.

A Go bag will contain the following items:

  • Backpack
  • Toiletry Kit
  • Blanket
  • Underwear, Socks, and a T-Shirt
  • Sweatshirt and Sweat Pants
  • Charging Cord
  • Pad of Paper and a Pencil

The Women's Bag Will Also Contain:

  • Sports Bra
  • Personal Hygiene Items

Please note that if you or your loved one are being held by orders of the U.S. Marshall Service, their rules prohibit us from providing a Go Bag.

Each Detainee that is being deported can either take a bag of belongings provided by their family, or a Go Bag. They cannot take both.

Only Detainees being held at the Chase County Detention Center in Cottonwood Falls, KS are eligible for this Go Bag.

October 2026 Month-End Report

This is the October, 2026 month end financial report for Love in Action, Inc. We began the month with $4,102.36. We received $2,840 in checks, $636.66 via VENMO, $228.36 via PayPal, $215 via Zeffy and $267 in cash. We also received 580 stamps at a value of $475.60 as well as $883 via Amazon. Two groups donated socks, sweat pants, T-Shirts and sweat shirts in addition.

We spent $1,700 for the Detainees phone cards that will be given to them fore their Christmas present, $2,542.04 on Go Bag supplies, $30 for an additional bag of Halloween candy and $53 for Sugar free Halloween candy. That leaves a balance of $3,964.34

The Center held a ribbon cutting ceremony to dedicate the new exercise equipment that Love in Action paid for half of the cost ($6,000). They took the opportunity to refresh the indoor yard with fresh paint and will be adding a mural sometime next month. We sent in a Trick or Treat Bag for all the Detainees that contained the traditional chocolate candy bars as well as some Mexican candy that was requested by some of the Detainees. We continue our Pen Pal program and we sent in 600 stamps in October for our card and stamp program. We also sent in some items requested by the Detainees and staff from our Amazon gift registry.

Our November treat will be a slice of cheesecake. The Sergeant that runs the card and stamp program for us asked the Detainee what they would enjoy for their monthly treat. Cheesecake was at the top of their list.

We have ordered $10 phone cards for each Detainee that will be given to them as a Christmas present. Some of the Detainees have not had the funds to call home since they have been detained, some for months. It warms my heart to think that their family will be able to hear their voice on Christmas thanks to all of you generous donors.

Our biggest focus of the month was preparing over 100 Go Bags. We held seven work days and volunteers came from all over the state to participate. We gathered and cut the tags off of all the goods and sorted them by size. We washed and folded the items. The volunteers also prepared the inner bags of feminine hygiene products as well as the baggie that contains a pad of paper, a pencil, a charging cord and our business card. We also filled the backpacks with all the material goods and a whole lot of love. The last work day we counted the leftovers and stored them out of my house.

I am leaving on vacation November 3 and will return November 16. When I get back, we will update our website with the information about how a family can request a Go Bag for their loved one that is scheduled to be deported. When the Center approves the request, I will take a bag to the Center, it will be inspected and then logged in as personal property for the Detainee. The bag will travel with the Detainee through their deportation process and will be given to them when they arrive at their final deportation country.

Each Go Bag backpack will contain a blanket, a toiletry kit, a pair of sweat pants and sweat shirt, a T-Shirt, underwear, two pairs of socks, a charging cord, a pad of paper and a pencil. The women will also receive a small supply of feminine hygiene products, 2 hair ties and a sports bra.

We have no idea yet what the demand for the bags will be. Family members can bring a bag for their loved one, but not all families can provide one. Detainees can have either a bag from their family or one of our Go Bags, they cannot take two bags with them on their deportation journey.

This Center has sent approximately 300 Detainees a month so far this year on their deportation journey. They estimate that about half of the families provide a bag. That means we could potentially need 150 bags a month. That number does fluctuate and they can’t predict if those numbers will hold, decrease or even increase.

If Love in Action has to purchase all the items for the bags, the cost is approximately $35 per bag or $3,500 for 100 bags. However, due to the generosity of our donors, we have enough leftovers that it will cost approximately $1,600 for our next set of 100 bags. We have the funds to cover that expense. If the demand for the bags is high and we need to act quickly, we have a team in place to make that happen so we can quickly put together another set of 100 bags.

After that, we will have to do some major fundraising to raise the funds to meet demand. However, we don’t yet know what that demand will be. Policy can change, the numbers can decrease, and we may not need so many or the demand may even increase and we will need more bags each month.

If the demand is high and stays high, there are grants that will be written to help with the funding. Because of this big increase in our budget we are lifting the $1,000 a month restriction that an individual donor can give each year to $5,000.

We shall see what happens and I will inform you of our needs when I know what they are. We are negotiating to have a new place to store and work from starting in December which will make the process much easier.

It is only because of your donations of cash, labor and purchases from the Amazon Gift Registry for Detainee supplies that this project has come together. Your support this year has been amazing and is deeply appreciated.

On each of our work days, we began with a meditation to dedicate our hands and hearts to the Detainees and to recognize the challenge of their journey. I would like to share that again with all of you.

May the work of our hands today bring a small measure of comfort, dignity, and safety to those who receive these bags. We acknowledge the difficulty of their journey and send our deepest wishes for their resilience, protection, and peace.

We pack these bags not just with physical necessities, but with a profound respect for the shared humanity of every person receiving them. May these items serve as a reminder that they are seen, valued, and wished well as they start their journey towards a new way of living.

May it be so. And so it is. Amen.

Past Projects

Through the generosity of supporters, Love In Action has been able to provide meaningful resources, encouragement, and practical support to detainees at the Chase County Detention Center.

  • Recreational equipment including basketballs, soccer balls, and a large piece of exercise equipment.
  • Playing cards, games, and puzzles.
  • Books and journals.
  • Handheld translators so staff can quickly meet the needs of detainees who speak many different languages.
  • Greeting cards, stationery, and stamps so detainees can send notes to their families and legal teams.
  • Summer events and celebrations, including Juneteenth, Father's Day, and July 4 activities with treats for detainees.
  • A first anniversary celebration at the Old School with lunch, music, cake, and cupcakes for detainees and guests.
  • Stamp drives and Pen Pal outreach that helped detainees stay connected with loved ones and legal teams.
  • Special treats and celebrations including Thanksgiving pizza, Christmas gift cards, World Cup treats, and other special events.
