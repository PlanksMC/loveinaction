What is Love In Action

Love In Action was formed by Kay Krause in July 2025 to support the 120 ICE detainees and the other 28 detainees being held at the Chase County Detention Center in Kansas.

Through compassion, encouragement, and practical support, Love In Action helps detainees stay connected while providing resources that bring comfort and hope.

July 2026 Month-End Report

This is the July, 2026 month end report for Love in Action. We started the month with $8,491.01.

We received $319.29 from VENMO donations, $80 in cash donations, and $380 in donations that came in via checks.

$153 worth of goods was purchased via our Amazon gift registry. We received 534 stamps via donations.

We spent $1,329.10 in stamps, special events for the Detainees, Pen Pal email fees, and the initial expense to set up our website.

We ended the month with $7,941.20 on hand with $6,000 of that still due to the Detention Center when the exercise equipment bill comes. The funds donated for the pizza event in August are not included in these numbers.

July Highlights

We provided wings with sauces to the Detainees for July 4th. On Love in Action’s birthday July 11, we gifted each Detainee a cupcake so they could celebrate with us. On July 19, each Detainee got a Coke and a bag of popcorn to enjoy while they watched the World Cup.

I hope you all saw the thank you notes the Detainees sent for these events.

Anniversary Celebration

On July 11, 25 donors gathered to celebrate Love in Action’s first year of service to the Detainees. It was a heart-filled day where the donors got to hear the history of Love in Action and great musical entertainment presented by Elexa Dawson.

The white boards that the Detainees signed and the Lieutenant brought to the party brought tears to my eyes. They are posted on our Facebook page so all can share in them. The Detainees really do appreciate all that Love in Action does for them.

Stamp Drive

The stamp drive has been very successful as we have received 834 stamps over the last two months. We will continue the stamp drive as an ongoing project.

Stamps can be mailed to Love In Action, Inc., 421 Plum St., Cottonwood Falls, KS 66845. At this time, we do not need any international stamps.

Pen Pal Program

Our Pen Pal program continues. We are currently writing to eight Detainees. I trust both parties in the Pen Pal program are finding it to be rewarding. It can be hard to be a volunteer though, as some of the life stories we hear can break your heart. However, I know that having someone that allows you to share your story and walk beside you can lighten the load for the Detainee.

Website Update

I am continuing to work on setting up a website for Love in Action. I am concerned that our only means of communication is via Facebook. We should launch the site sometime in August. Stay tuned for details!

August Pizza Event

Every year on my birthday I throw myself a party. My birthday is August 21 and this year I want to give the Detainees pizza to help me celebrate. It costs about $800 for enough pizza for all the Detainees and the staff that are working when it is served. I have a Facebook Fundraiser happening to help raise the funds to make this happen. What isn’t raised, I will donate.

Center Update

The Center is currently overloaded with Detainees as the current government’s administration continues its policy of detaining anyone they can find that is undocumented. The Center has been averaging 170 Detainees daily. Their official capacity is 148.

Succession Planning

I think every successful nonprofit needs a succession plan and I want to start putting one into place. If you might be interested in taking over my role or at least a part of it, please call me and let’s talk. I have no immediate plans to step aside but I do want to start putting a plan in place for when that day comes.

Released Detainee Support

I transported a released ICE Detainee on July 30. Her birth country is China and she currently lives in New York. I helped her get to KC where she needed to pick up her ID from the Department of Homeland Security in KCMO and then catch a flight home to New York. She had no cash on her when she was picked up.

Love in Action steps in to help fill the gap when Detainees get released. Her story is posted on the Love in Action Facebook page.

Kindness

Thank you for sharing your acts of kindness and love with Love in Action so the Detainees can feel your love and kindness.

Past Projects

Through the generosity of supporters, Love In Action has been able to provide meaningful resources, encouragement, and practical support to detainees at the Chase County Detention Center.

  • Recreational equipment including basketballs, soccer balls, and a large piece of exercise equipment.
  • Playing cards, games, and puzzles.
  • Books and journals.
  • Handheld translators so staff can quickly meet the needs of detainees who speak many different languages.
  • Greeting cards, stationery, and stamps so detainees can send notes to their families and legal teams.
  • Summer events and celebrations, including Juneteenth, Father's Day, and July 4 activities with treats for detainees.
  • A first anniversary celebration at the Old School with lunch, music, cake, and cupcakes for detainees and guests.
  • Stamp drives and Pen Pal outreach that helped detainees stay connected with loved ones and legal teams.
  • Special treats and celebrations including Thanksgiving pizza, Christmas gift cards, World Cup treats, and other special events.
