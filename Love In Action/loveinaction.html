What is Love In Action

Love In Action was formed by Kay Krause in July 2025 to support the 120 ICE detainees and the other 28 detainees being held at the Chase County Detention Center in Kansas.

Through compassion, encouragement, and practical support, Love In Action helps detainees stay connected while providing resources that bring comfort and hope.

June 2026 Month-End Report

We began June with $7,159.90 on hand. Our summer event fundraiser raised $2,605.71.

We spent $1,274.60 on stamps, a TV, summer events, and Pen Pal emails. We ended the month with $8,491.01.

We still owe the Detention Center money for our half of the exercise equipment, approximately $6,000, but payment has not yet been received.

We also received 300 stamps plus approximately $335 worth of goods sent to the Center through Amazon.

Summer Events

The Juneteenth summer event and Father's Day event, featuring Coke and M&M's, were well received by the Detainees.

Love In Action provided chicken wings that will be cooked and served by the Center for the Detainees on July 4.

The summer fundraiser surpassed the original goal of $1,000 by raising $2,605.71.

First Anniversary Celebration

Love In Action will celebrate its first year of existence on July 11, 2026.

The celebration will be held at the Old School located at 518 3rd St. in Cottonwood Falls, KS at noon.

  • Lunch will be served.
  • Musical entertainment by Elexa Dawson.
  • Cake and ice cream will be provided.
  • The Detainees will receive a cupcake to celebrate.
  • Everyone is invited to this family-friendly event.

Past Projects

Through the generosity of supporters, Love In Action has been able to provide meaningful resources, encouragement, and practical support to detainees at the Chase County Detention Center.

  • Recreational equipment including basketballs, soccer balls, and a large piece of exercise equipment.
  • Playing cards, games, and puzzles.
  • Books and journals.
  • Handheld translators so staff can quickly meet the needs of detainees who speak many different languages.
  • Greeting cards, stationery, and stamps so detainees can send notes to their families and legal teams.
  • Summer events and celebrations, including Juneteenth, Father's Day, and July 4 activities with treats for detainees.
  • A first anniversary celebration at the Old School with lunch, music, cake, and cupcakes for detainees and guests.
  • Stamp drives and Pen Pal outreach that helped detainees stay connected with loved ones and legal teams.
  • Special treats and celebrations including Thanksgiving pizza, Christmas gift cards, World Cup treats, and other special events.
