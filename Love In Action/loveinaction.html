Family Support Information

Love In Action was formed by Kay Krause in July 2025 to support the 120 ICE detainees being held at the Chase County Detention Center in Kansas. Through compassion, encouragement, and practical support, Love In Action helps detainees stay connected while providing resources that bring comfort and hope.

What is Love In Action?

Love In Action exists to care for detainees with compassion, dignity, and practical support. The organization helps people stay connected through cards, stamps, encouragement through a Pen Pal program, while also providing simple comforts and meaningful support.

🤝 Volunteer

The only hands-on volunteer opportunity is to be a Pen PAL with a Detainee. The Center uses between 400 - 600 stamps a month which we provide.

  • Stamps can be mailed to 421 Plum St., Cottonwood Falls, KS 66845.
  • Funds are greatly appreciated to help support the special events we do for the Detainees monthly, pay for the Pen PAL program fees, and pay for stamps.
  • When the Center has specific needs, they are listed on the Amazon Gift Registry.
  • Gifts purchased from the registry go directly to the Center.
  • One of the most valuable ways you can volunteer is to bear witness to the stories we share which tell the life stories of the Detainees. Please allow those stories to be felt in your heart, then allow them to soften your heart and find ways to show compassion to all you encounter.

Past Projects

  • Recreational equipment including basketballs, soccer balls, and a large piece of exercise equipment.
  • Playing cards, games, and puzzles.
  • Books and journals.
  • Handheld translators so the staff can quickly meet the needs of the Detainees that speak many different languages.
  • Greeting cards, stationery, and stamps so the Detainees can send notes to their families and legal teams weekly.
  • Special treats on holidays such as pizza for Thanksgiving, gift cards at Christmas, and Coke and popcorn for World Cup day.
